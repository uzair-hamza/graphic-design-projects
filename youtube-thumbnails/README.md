# YouTube Thumbnails

Three 1280×720 thumbnails (the standard YouTube thumbnail size), built
for high contrast and readability at small sizes: big bold title text
with a black outline over a dark gradient, so the words stay readable
on any screen.

## Files
- `thumb_badcaty.jpg` — Bad Caty funny cat video.
  Title: "BAD CATY GOES WILD!" (yellow text).
- `thumb_dragon.jpg` — Dragon Dost story video.
  Title: "THE BABY DRAGON'S LAST FRIEND" (white text).
- `thumb_ai.jpg` — Tech/AI explainer video.
  Title: "HOW AI REALLY LEARNS" (light-blue text).

## Fonts
- Titles: DejaVu Sans Bold, ~118 px, with a 7 px black stroke.

## Tools
- Background scenes: AI-generated artwork (no text in the artwork).
- All titles, gradients, and layout: composed with Python (Pillow).
